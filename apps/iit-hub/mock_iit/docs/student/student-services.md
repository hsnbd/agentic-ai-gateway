# Student services

- **Advising**: every student has a faculty adviser; book a slot through the
  academic office.
- **Library**: open 8:00 to 20:00, including online access to IEEE and ACM
  digital libraries with your student email.
- **Labs**: the software lab and the networking lab are open to students
  outside class hours with a student ID card.
- **Internships**: BSSE students in the fourth year are placed with partner
  companies for six months; the internship office runs a matching fair in
  October.
