# Enrollment rules (students)

- A student may carry at most **18 credits** in one semester.
- A course can be taken only after its **prerequisite** has been completed.
  For example, SE 302 Software Testing requires SE 201 Data Structures.
- Students may only enroll in courses of their own program.
- Courses can be added or dropped until the add/drop deadline (24 January 2027
  for Spring 2027). After that, withdrawing needs the academic office's
  approval and is recorded as W on the transcript.
- When a course is full, the student may ask the academic office to be placed
  on its waiting list.
- The hub chatbot can enroll you or drop a course for you, but it always asks
  you to confirm before it changes anything.
