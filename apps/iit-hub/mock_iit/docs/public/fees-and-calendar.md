# Fees and academic calendar

## Tuition
- BSSE: 2,500 BDT per credit.
- MSSE: 4,000 BDT per credit.
- MIT: 5,000 BDT per credit.
Fees are paid per semester at enrollment. Merit scholarships cover up to 50% of
tuition for students with a semester GPA of 3.75 or above.

## Calendar (Spring 2027 semester)
- Classes start: 10 January 2027.
- Last day to add or drop a course: 24 January 2027.
- Midterm examinations: 7 to 14 March 2027.
- Final examinations: 2 to 16 May 2027.
- Results published: 6 June 2027.
