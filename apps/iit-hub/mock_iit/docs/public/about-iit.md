# About IIT

The Institute of Information Technology (IIT) is an academic institute offering
undergraduate and graduate programs in software engineering and information
technology. This is demonstration content for the IIT information hub.

## Contact
- Academic office: academic@iit.example.edu, Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 to 17:00.
- Admission desk: admission@iit.example.edu.
- The office is on the ground floor of the IIT building, room 101.

## Programs
- **BSSE** — Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering. Four years, eight
  semesters, 160 credits including a six-month industry internship in the
  final year.
- **MSSE** — Master of Science in Software Engineering. Two years, 48 credits,
  thesis or project track.
- **MIT** — Master in Information Technology. A professional program for
  working graduates, 40 credits, classes on Friday and Saturday.
