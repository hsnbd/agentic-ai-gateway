# Admission

## Undergraduate (BSSE)
Applications for BSSE open every January and close at the end of February.
Applicants need a higher secondary certificate with mathematics and physics.
Selection is by a written admission test held in March, followed by a viva.
The application fee is 1,000 BDT.

## Graduate (MSSE and MIT)
Graduate applications open twice a year, in January and in July. Applicants
need a four-year bachelor's degree; MSSE requires a degree in a computing
discipline, while MIT accepts any discipline with two years of work experience.
Selection is by written test and interview.

## Enrolling through the hub
Prospective students can enroll in an entry-level course of their program
directly through the IIT hub chatbot: give your full name, email address and
program, choose a course, and confirm. You are admitted provisionally and
receive a student ID; the academic office verifies your documents within two
weeks. Entry-level courses are those without a prerequisite, for example
SE 101 and SE 102 (BSSE), SE 501 (MSSE), and MIT 501 or MIT 502 (MIT).
