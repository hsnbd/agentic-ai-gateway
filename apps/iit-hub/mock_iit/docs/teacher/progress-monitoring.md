# Monitoring program progress (faculty)

The hub gives teachers class-level progress for the courses they teach and a
per-batch summary for the programs they teach in.

- A student is **at risk** in a course when their attendance is below 75% or
  their running average mark is below 50.
- Course coordinators review at-risk lists every two weeks and refer students
  with two or more at-risk courses to their faculty adviser.
- Batch summaries report average attendance, average marks, and the number of
  at-risk enrollments per batch; the program coordinator presents them to the
  academic council each month.
- Student-level records are confidential. Teachers see individual progress
  only for students in their own courses, and must not share it outside the
  teaching team.
