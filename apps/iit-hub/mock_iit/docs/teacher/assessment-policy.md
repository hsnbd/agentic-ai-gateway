# Assessment policy (faculty)

- Each course has at least **six assessments** per semester: four class tests
  or assignments, a midterm, and a final examination.
- Marks for class tests and assignments must be published to students within
  two weeks of the assessment.
- Attendance is recorded every class. Students below 75% attendance are barred
  from the final examination; teachers must warn them in writing after week 8.
- Midterm marks are submitted to the examination committee one week after the
  midterm period ends; final marks within ten days of the final examination.
